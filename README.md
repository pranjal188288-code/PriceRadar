 The rapid growth of online shopping has made it difficult for consumers to identify the best price for a product across different e-commerce platforms. 
 The same product may be available at different prices on Amazon, Flipkart, Snapdeal, and Meesho, while discounts, coupons, bank offers, and delivery options may further affect the final purchasing decision. To address this problem, PriceRadar is developed as a multiplatform price comparison and tracking application.
